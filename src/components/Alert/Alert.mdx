## Alert Component

The Alert component displays a short, important message without interrupting the user's task. It is built on top of Material-UI's Alert, offering styled variations for different severities and actions.

## Overview

Alerts provide feedback to the user, typically about the status of their actions or system conditions. They come in several severities (success, info, warning, and error) and standard layout styles.
