## PromotionCard Component

The PromotionCard component is a full-bleed promotional banner card built from MUI primitives. It displays a background image, an overlaid title, and a configurable call-to-action button.

## Overview

Use PromotionCard for homepage banners, featured campaign slots, or any placement where a product or promotion needs visual impact. The title and button are anchored in the top-left of the card, overlaid on the background image — matching the standard e-commerce promotional banner layout.

## Design tokens

PromotionCard is a composite component with no single MUI counterpart, so its tokens resolve to a plain style object (via <code>promotionCardStyles</code>) consumed through <code>sx</code> props rather than a registered MUI component override.
