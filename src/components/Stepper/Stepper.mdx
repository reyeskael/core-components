## Stepper Component

The Stepper component displays progress through a sequence of logical and numbered steps. It is built on top of Material-UI's Stepper components, providing a simplified and styled interface.

## Overview

Steppers display progress through a sequence of steps. They are particularly useful for multi-step forms, wizards, or guiding users through a structured process.
