## Accordion Component

The Accordion component is a collapsible panel built on top of Material-UI's Accordion, pairing a always-visible summary (e.g. a question) with details that expand and collapse on click (e.g. an answer).

## Overview

Accordions let users scan a list of topics and reveal only the content they care about — commonly used for FAQs, settings groups, and other progressively-disclosed content.
